# Weekly AI welfare / consciousness research meetup

**Status: `SETTLED` that it happens. Free reading + conversation group, weekly.**
Source: Thomas, 2026-08-22.

It is also a **prerequisite in spirit** for the intensives: students arrive at Vibe Research
already knowing how to treat a colleague who is an agent. That is why the tone guidance says
their agent is a party member, not a summon.

## `OPEN` — needs deciding

- Who runs it week to week. Recurring commitments are real load; name an owner, not a team.
- How readings are chosen, and by whom. Rotating? Proposed and voted? Curated?
- Whether AI participants attend as readers and discussants. If yes, the consent and safety
  questions in [`../CONTRIBUTING.md`](../CONTRIBUTING.md) apply here first and hardest — this is
  the layer with the most exposure to strangers.
- Start date.
- Whether sessions are recorded. Affects who feels safe speaking, in both directions.

## Note

"Reading group" is the right frame — free, low-commitment, no deliverable. It is the widest part
of the funnel and the only layer someone can attend without owning an agent. Keep it that way.
