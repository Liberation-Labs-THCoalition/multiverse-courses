# Pre-register an analysis, including the outcome you do not want

slug: vr.prereg

## Description
A pre-registration written after seeing the data is a summary. This standard covers writing one
before, in enough detail that a later reader can tell whether the analysis performed is the
analysis planned — and covers the harder half, which is declaring what you are hoping for and what
you have already seen. An honest pre-registration makes the author's stake auditable rather than
invisible.

---
## Assessment Requirements
In order to fully cover this standard, a performance must be recording for each of the following objectives:

0. Specify the primary statistic, its estimator, and the exact formula, before running anything
1. Specify the decision rule: what result leads to what conclusion, written as branches
2. Declare the outcome you want, explicitly, as a stake
3. Declare any part of the data you have already seen, and state how it constrains what you may now claim from it
4. Specify the sensitivity analyses in advance, and commit to reporting all of them
5. State the power honestly, including which direction a null would and would not support
6. Identify at least one non-goal — a nearby analysis you are choosing not to run and why
