# Research accelerator

**Status: `OPEN`. Starting next year.** Source: Thomas, 2026-08-22.

Match people already actively working on research with **mentors from Liz's network** and
**compute from the school**.

## What is settled

- It starts next year, not now.
- The school supplies the network and the compute.
- It is for people *already doing* research, not people learning to.

## `OPEN` — everything else

- Our role versus the school's. What does "support" commit Liberation Labs to, in hours?
- Selection: rolling applications against the school's communal student body, or cohorts?
- Whether completing the Vibe Research intensives is a prerequisite or merely a signal.
- Who mentors. Whether any Coalition member is a mentor, and by consent.
- What compute, and who administers the grant of it.
